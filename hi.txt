🇮🇳 Patent Registration in India — Step by Step
1.	Create your invention
Make sure you have a clear technical in	vention, not just a general idea. 
2.	Check patentability
Your invention should generally be new, inventive, and industrially applicable. 
3.	Do a patent search
Search existing patents to check whether a similar invention has already been published. 
4.	Keep your invention confidential
Avoid publicly revealing the invention before filing the patent application. 
5.	Prepare the patent specification
Explain: 
o	Title of invention 
o	Problem being solved 
o	How the invention works 
o	Advantages 
o	Drawings, if required 
o	Claims describing the protection you seek 
6.	Choose the application type 
o	Provisional application — when the invention is still being developed. 
o	Complete application — when the invention is fully developed. 
7.	Create an IP India account
Use the official IP India website. 
8.	File the patent application online
Submit the required forms and specification through the official patent e-filing system.
Patent e-Filing Portal 
9.	Pay the government fee
Pay the applicable patent filing fee. 
10.	Request examination
Submit the required request for examination so the Patent Office examines your application. 
11.	Respond to objections
If the Patent Office raises objections, submit your response and make permitted amendments. 
12.	Patent grant
If all requirements are satisfied, the Indian Patent Office grants the patent.

🇮🇳 Copyright Registration — Step by Step
Step 1: Identify your work
Decide what you want to register:
•	Software/source code 
•	Website 
•	Book 
•	Drawing/design 
•	Music 
•	Video 
•	Photography 
Step 2: Prepare the work
Keep a complete copy of your original work ready. For software, this can include the source code and relevant documentation.
Step 3: Create an account
Go to the official Copyright Office website and use the online registration facility.
Step 4: Fill the application
Enter:
•	Applicant name and address 
•	Author details 
•	Type of work 
•	Title of the work 
•	Date of creation/publication, if applicable 
•	Ownership details 
Step 5: Upload required documents
Upload the work and supporting documents requested for your particular category.
Step 6: Pay the registration fee
Pay the applicable government fee online. The amount depends on the category of work.
Step 7: Submit the application
Submit the application and save your Diary/Application Number.
Step 8: Examination/objection period
The Copyright Office examines the application. If an objection or discrepancy is raised, you may need to provide a response or clarification.
Step 9: Registration
If the application satisfies the requirements, the work is registered and the registration certificate is issued.
